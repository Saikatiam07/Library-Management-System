PLEASE READ

Library Rules & Guide

These simple rules keep books available and fair for every student.

1
Borrow up to 5 books

Each physical copy counts separately. You may issue multiple copies of one title if copies are available.

2
Free for 15 days

Return within the due date to pay nothing. A fine starts only after the free period ends.

3
Renew once for 7 extra days

Renewal is not allowed after the due date or when another student is waiting for the book.

4
Reserve unavailable books

When all copies are issued, join the queue. The first student in the queue gets priority.

5
Overdue fine: ₹2 per day

Return the book first; any overdue fine can then be collected by the library.

6
Return the correct copy

My Books shows every issued copy separately, so return or renew the exact book record you selected.
